Privacy Policy — SHIFT IN PROGRESS

Effective date: 5 July 2026

SHIFT IN PROGRESS is published by SludgePup Games.

Data collection

SHIFT IN PROGRESS does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The game does not require an account, login, internet connection, location access, camera access, microphone access, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Game progress, scores, settings, and unlocks may be stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to SludgePup Games or any third party.

Children’s privacy

SHIFT IN PROGRESS does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Third-party services

The current version of SHIFT IN PROGRESS does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection services.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
